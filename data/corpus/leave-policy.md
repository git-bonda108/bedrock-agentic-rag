# Leave and Time Off Policy

Owner: People Operations (#ask-people).

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. PTO accrues monthly.

Up to 5 unused PTO days can be carried over into the next year and must be used by March 31; any other unused days expire at the end of the year.

Requests for more than 5 consecutive days should be submitted at least two weeks in advance and approved by your manager.

## Sick leave

Employees have 10 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO. No medical note is needed for absences of up to 3 consecutive days; longer absences require a doctor's note.

## Parental leave

All new parents, including adoptive and foster parents, receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement. A phased return at 60% hours for up to 4 weeks is available afterwards.

## Other leave

Bereavement leave is 5 paid days for the loss of an immediate family member and 2 days for other relatives. Employees also receive 2 paid volunteering days per year.
