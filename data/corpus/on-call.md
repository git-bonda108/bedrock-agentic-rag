# On-Call Policy

Owner: Engineering Operations. Applies to engineers on a production on-call rotation.

## Rotation

Rotations are weekly and hand over on Monday at 10:00 local time of the primary engineer. Each rotation has a primary and a secondary. The secondary is paged if the primary does not acknowledge within the severity's acknowledgement window.

No engineer may be primary for more than 2 consecutive weeks, and new engineers shadow at least one full rotation before joining as secondary.

## Compensation

On-call engineers receive a stipend of $400 per week as primary and $150 per week as secondary, paid with the following month's payroll.

If you are paged between 00:00 and 06:00 local time, you are entitled to a compensatory day off, to be taken within 30 days. Record it in the HR system as "on-call recovery".

## Handover

The outgoing primary writes a handover note in #oncall-handover covering open incidents, ongoing risks and noisy alerts. Alerts that fire more than 3 times in a week without action must be tuned or deleted; this is reviewed at each handover.

## Swaps and absences

Swaps are arranged between engineers and recorded in the scheduling tool at least 24 hours in advance. If you are sick during your rotation, notify your manager and the secondary immediately; the secondary becomes primary.
