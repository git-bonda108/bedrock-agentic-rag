# Expenses and Travel Policy

Owner: Finance (#ask-finance). All expenses are submitted and approved in Ramp.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expenses within 30 days of purchase. Itemised receipts are required for any expense over $25. Expenses over $1,000 need pre-approval from your manager before purchase.

## Meals

The daily meal limit while travelling is $75 for domestic trips and $100 for international trips, covering breakfast, lunch and dinner combined. Alcohol is not reimbursed except at approved team events.

## Flights

Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer; business class requires VP approval. Book through the company travel portal at least 14 days in advance where possible.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is $250, or $350 in New York, San Francisco and London. Stays longer than 7 nights should use serviced apartments when cheaper.

## Remote work equipment

Employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $1,000 for furniture and equipment, plus $50 per month towards internet costs. Laptops and monitors are provided by IT and remain company property.

## Corporate cards

Corporate cards are for business expenses only. Personal charges must be reported and repaid within 7 days.
